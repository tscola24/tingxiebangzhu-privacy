Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

听写帮助 is a Chinese dictation practice app. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you.

Data stored on your device

Word sets, quiz settings, and theme preferences are saved locally on your device using standard device storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Camera and photo library

When you use the photo import feature, the image you select is sent to Google Cloud Vision API for Chinese text recognition. We do not store or retain this image. It is used solely to extract text and is not associated with you in any way.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Cloud Privacy Notice.

Text-to-speech

Pronunciation audio is generated by Google's text-to-speech service. Chinese text is sent to Google to produce audio playback. This is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Microphone

The app declares microphone permission for technical compatibility reasons. The app does not record, process, or transmit any audio from your microphone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, the app is safe for users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
